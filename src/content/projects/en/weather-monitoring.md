---
title: AgroClima Jalapa (graduation project)
problem: Farmers in Jalapa’s seven municipalities need advance warning of weather risks to their crops, even where mobile coverage is unreliable.
result: The Android app checks the forecast for each plot every three hours, sends automatic alerts when it detects risks and displays saved data offline.
tools: [Flutter, Firebase, GitHub Actions, Node.js, OpenWeather]
order: 1
demo: https://stevenquintanagt.github.io/Pag_Agroclima/
repo: https://github.com/StevenQuintanaGT/Agroclima_Jalapa
