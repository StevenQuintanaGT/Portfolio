---
title: AgroClima Jalapa (graduation project)
problem: Farmers in the 7 municipalities of Jalapa, Guatemala, need early warning when the forecast for their plot goes beyond what their crop can handle, often with weak phone signal.
result: An Android app with per-plot forecasts and automatic alerts every 3 hours. It runs at zero cost without its own server, on Firebase's free plan plus a scheduled GitHub Actions job, and keeps working offline with cached data.
tools: [Flutter, Firebase, GitHub Actions, Node.js, OpenWeather]
order: 1
demo: https://stevenquintanagt.github.io/Pag_Agroclima/
repo: https://github.com/StevenQuintanaGT/Agroclima_Jalapa
---
